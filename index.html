Louieverse Rankings

The official unofficial Louieverse character board. Rankings are based mainly on lore importance, activity, aura, loyalty, meme value, influence, and impact on the timeline.

Ranking Method

This ranking is mostly about lore importance, not just looks or chad stats. Founders, OG members, major conflict figures, loyal defenders, and characters who changed the timeline rank higher. Physical stats, meme value, activity, and aura still matter, but they do not automatically beat major story impact.
